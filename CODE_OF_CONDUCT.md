# Code of conduct

Servik follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be welcoming and patient, whatever someone's experience, language or
  background. Many of us write in a second language; English and Turkish are
  both fine in issues.
- Be kind and constructive. Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, personal or
  political attacks, or publishing others' private information.
- Assume good intent, and accept a maintainer's decision on what belongs in
  the project.

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and
anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, report it privately to the maintainers:
use the **Report content** option on the comment or issue in GitHub, or
contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in
confidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this
code, and may warn, suspend or ban people from the project, following the
Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
