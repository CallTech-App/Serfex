Serfex got its real logo, and the sidebar became simpler.

- **New logo:** instead of the three placeholder lines, a two-part S: the panel and the agent on the server meet in the middle. The same logo is in the sidebar, on the sign-in, setup and password pages, in the setup wizard and in the README. The browser tab now shows the Serfex icon (`favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, and `apple-touch-icon.png` when added to a phone's home screen).
- **Sidebar:** in the light theme the menu is light too, in the dark theme dark. Group headings are not in capitals, icons are thin and grey, and the selected page is marked with a thin teal line on the left. Problem counts are a small light-orange label instead of a filled orange circle. Menu names, order and groups are the same.
- **Version number:** moved from the top corner of the menu to the very bottom, under the user, and is visible only to administrators.
