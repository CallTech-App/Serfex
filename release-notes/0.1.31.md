- A new **Account details** page (click your name at the bottom left). Administrators, customers and team members can change their own full name, email address and language; customers their phone number too. The language choice moved here from Account security.
- **Changing the email address** asks for the current password and sends a confirmation link to the new address. The old address stays in effect until the link is opened and "Confirm address" is pressed, so a mistyped address does not lock the account. The link is valid for 24 hours; it can be sent again or cancelled. After the change, a notification email goes to the old address and the change is written to the activity log.
- The new address is also applied where the old address was used: notification recipients, the Let's Encrypt contact address and, for a customer account, the customer record. Password reset links sent to the old address stop working.
- If the panel cannot send email (no mail server or SMTP), the address changes immediately without confirmation; the dialog says so beforehand.
- **Change password** was added to **Account security**: current password and new password (at least 12 characters for administrators, at least 10 for everyone else). This browser stays signed in, other sessions are signed out, and the person gets a notification email.
- An administrator working as a customer cannot change the customer's email or password here; the address is changed from the Customers page.
