- Site logs are now rotated and compressed every night: sites' Nginx access and error logs, the Apache error log and the PHP error log, and the logs of the panel and webmail. A busy site no longer fills the disk over time.
- How many days logs are kept is chosen on Settings → Server (14 days by default, between 7 and 365 days).
- Lines written to the access log at the moment of rotation are also counted in statistics and live monitoring.
- Existing sites are added to the rule right after the update; they do not need to be saved again.
