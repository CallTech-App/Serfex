Jobs cut off by a panel update or a restart no longer stay stuck.

- **Sites stuck at "Deleting":** if a site deletion (a deletion with backup runs in the backup queue) was running at the moment a panel update stopped the job queues, the job was cut off halfway. The queue treated this job as "reserved" for 12 hours and then quietly dropped it. The site stayed at "Deleting" forever, with no error record. Now every job holds a lock while it runs. A job whose lock has been released but that still looks "reserved" is marked failed within a minute. The site moves to the "Error" state, and its page shows a "The site could not be deleted" warning with a **Retry deletion** button. The partial backup taken during the deletion is marked "failed" too and no longer blocks updates. A site creation cut off halfway also shows "Error" with "Try again". These jobs are also listed in `php artisan queue:failed`.
- **Updates wait for long jobs:** while a site deletion, move, copy or scan is running in the background, an update does not start and the reason is shown. If such a job starts after the update was requested, the update waits up to 15 minutes for it to finish before stopping the job queues.
- **Agent log:** while reading PostgreSQL connections, Live Monitor opened and closed a session as the `postgres` user every two seconds, filling the `serfex-agent` log with `pam_unix` lines. Now the query runs with the same privileges without opening a session (`setpriv`).
