The "500 Something went wrong" error that appeared now and then when adding a site was fixed.

- **Add site:** when a site is added, the panel queues a job that tells the server the addresses that may send email. If the same job was already waiting in the queue and had not run yet (for example when another site, subdomain or mail domain had just been added and the queue was busy with another job), PostgreSQL aborted the save halfway and "Add site" gave a 500 error. Now job locks are taken on a separate connection to the panel database, and the job is queued after the save completes; the site is added without trouble, and the waiting job sees the new site too.
- **Other operations of the same kind:** adding a subdomain, a redirect or a mail domain, changing a domain, and DNS changes could fail for the same reason; they all work with this fix.
- **Stuck lock:** the lock of a job deleted from the queue now blocks new notifications for at most 10 minutes instead of a day; the hourly check also keeps fixing this separately.
