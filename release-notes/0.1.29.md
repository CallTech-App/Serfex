- DNSSEC: Settings → "Sign the zone" on the domain's DNS page. The zone is signed with an ECDSA P-256 key (algorithm 13), names that do not exist are proven with NSEC3, and records changed later are signed too. Once it is on, the page shows, ready to copy, the DS record to enter at the domain registrar (key tag, algorithm, digest type 2 / SHA-256, digest) and the public key (DNSKEY) for registrars that ask for it. The domain list has a DNSSEC column.
- Turning DNSSEC off asks for confirmation (delete the DS record at the registrar first). The key is stored encrypted in the panel and in site backups: turning it on again, bringing a deleted zone back with "Undo", or restoring the site from a backup (on another server too) brings back the same key, so the DS record at the registrar stays valid.
- Secondary DNS: Domains & DNS → Server DNS settings → "Secondary name servers". The IP addresses/networks entered can transfer zones (AXFR), and single addresses are notified of every change immediately (NOTIFY). Zones are listed in a catalog zone (RFC 9432); secondary servers such as BIND 9.18+, PowerDNS 4.7+ and Knot add new domains on their own within a minute and remove deleted ones. The page shows BIND and PowerDNS configuration prepared for this server. Signed zones are transferred with their signatures.
- "Zone transfer permission" in Services → PowerDNS → Settings now only shows the list and links to Server DNS settings; changes are made in one place. Addresses entered in the earlier release are kept, and on the first "Save and apply" the zones are opened to the secondary servers.
