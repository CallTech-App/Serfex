Customers can now be deleted from the customer list: select them and press "Delete". Until now the only way to delete a customer was the "..." menu on the customer's own page, so selecting a customer in the list offered only "Suspend".

- **Delete from the list:** select one or more customers and press "Delete (n)". The dialog lists who goes, offers "Also delete their sites" (with the number of sites), and asks for the customer's system user to be typed, or, for several customers, how many there are.
- **Same deletion as before:** the customers are deleted after the 10-minute undo window, exactly as from the customer's page. Meanwhile their sites show the suspension page and the list shows when each one goes for good, with "Undo" on the row and on the notification. Without "Also delete their sites" the sites stay on the server and serve again without an owner.
- A customer who was suspended by mistake while looking for delete can be deleted the same way; nothing else needs to be undone first.
- Fixed: suspending several customers at once showed "2 customer suspended.|2 customers suspended." in English instead of the plural form.
